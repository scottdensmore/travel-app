## Suggestions

**4. Blocked and ready are a 1.54:1 colour change and nothing else.** `#4c1d95` vs `#6d28d9`, same label, same size, `opacity: 1`. `cursor: not-allowed` never happens on touch, and `aria-disabled` is assistive-tech only, so a sighted mouse or touch user gets no signal from the button at all. This is the direct answer to your question: **before pressing it reads as neither blocked nor broken — it reads as ready.** After pressing it reads as blocked, clearly, because the alert is genuinely good. The mitigation is real (the unticked box sits right above it and is visible at every viewport), so I would not call this a 1.4.1 failure — but the honest cursor you added is invisible to most of the people in that state. Side-by-side: `detail-phone-attestation-and-blocked-button.png` vs `detail-phone-attestation-and-ready-button.png`.

**5. Nothing ever confirms the attestation was recorded.** The panel just disappears. A customer returning for the return leg cannot tell whether they confirmed. `documentsConfirmedAt` is already selected on the customer projection (`app/checkin/page.tsx:126-135`) and is a timestamp, so "Traveller details confirmed on 18 Aug" is showable under the policy — it names no category value at all.

**6. Checkbox is 18.39px (1.15rem), under WCAG 2.5.8's 24x24 minimum.** It passes via the exception, because the `&lt;label for&gt;` is an equivalent target (253x110 at phone, 839x44 at desktop) and clicking the text works — I verified both the box and the label text toggle it. Nearest other target is ~130px away. Growing the box to 24px or padding the hit area would remove the need to lean on the exception.

**7. Unchecked box renders as a solid white square** — the UA light-scheme control against a dark panel. It is loud and at a glance can read as filled rather than empty. `color-scheme: dark` on the root would make it dark-with-border and unambiguous. Checked state is unambiguous (purple box, white tick).

**8. Marginal, not a failure:** the checked fill `#7c3aed` against the composited panel `rgb(34,33,41)` is **2.78:1**, just under 3:1 for non-text contrast. The state itself is carried by the white tick at 5.7:1, so 1.4.11 is satisfied — worth knowing only because it is close.
